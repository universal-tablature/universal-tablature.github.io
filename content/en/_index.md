  <section class="utab-purpose" aria-labelledby="purpose-heading">
    <div class="utab-section-heading">
      <p class="utab-eyebrow">One score, many uses</p>
      <h2 id="purpose-heading">Musical intent that travels</h2>
      <p>Audio is faithful but heavy and difficult to edit. MIDI is compact, but often loses the physical and musical intent behind a note. Universal Tablature keeps that intent explicit.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="utab-card-grid">
      <article class="utab-card">
        <span class="utab-card-mark" aria-hidden="true">01</span>
        <h3>Compose without the baggage</h3>
        <p>Store musical structure instead of large rendered media. Edit, rearrange, and regenerate notation or playback whenever you need it.</p>
      </article>
      <article class="utab-card">
        <span class="utab-card-mark" aria-hidden="true">02</span>
        <h3>Train on any instrument</h3>
        <p>Build learning tools that understand strings, keys, holes, valves, drum surfaces, or entirely new physical interfaces.</p>
      </article>
      <article class="utab-card">
        <span class="utab-card-mark" aria-hidden="true">03</span>
        <h3>Drive physical playback</h3>
        <p>Use explicit actuator targets for virtual instruments, mechanical installations, accessible interfaces, and robotic performers.</p>
      </article>
      <article class="utab-card">
        <span class="utab-card-mark" aria-hidden="true">04</span>
        <h3>Exchange without starting over</h3>
        <p>Move between Universal Tablature, MusicXML, MEI, MNX, and LilyPond, or render MIDI directly from the same source.</p>
      </article>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="utab-flow" aria-labelledby="flow-heading">
    <div>
      <p class="utab-eyebrow">A practical pipeline</p>
      <h2 id="flow-heading">Write once. Resolve precisely. Use anywhere.</h2>
    </div>
    <ol class="utab-flow-list">
      <li><strong>Author in <code>.utab</code></strong><span>Write human-readable musical intent with reusable phrases, voices, parts, and arrangements.</span></li>
      <li><strong>Resolve to <code>.utab.json</code></strong><span>Compile it into precise timing, instrument setup, techniques, and playable events.</span></li>
      <li><strong>Make something with it</strong><span>Learn, edit, render, analyze, perform, or export MIDI and notation from the same musical source.</span></li>
    </ol>
  </section>
