These tutorials develop complete Composer Language scores and explain how their
modules, phrases, sections, voices, and arrangements fit together.

link:/en/tutorials/twinkle/[Arrange “Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star”]::
Build a complete 12-bar arrangement for piano, two guitars, and voice using
scale-relative melody phrases, chords, lyrics, and repetition.

link:/en/tutorials/hurrian-hymn/[Encode a reconstruction of Hurrian Hymn No. 6]::
Represent a nine-bar reconstruction for the standard-library sammûm while
keeping editorial choices visible in the source.

link:/en/tutorials/feature-tour/[Tour expressive timing and harmony]::
Use an original six-bar study to explore tuplets, ornaments,
dynamics, tempo ramps, altered harmony, and pedal state.

link:/en/tutorials/example-library/[Public-domain and original example scores]::
Download fresh UTAB transcriptions of Beethoven and Bach excerpts alongside an
original feature study, with provenance and reuse guidance.

link:/en/tutorials/percussion-groove/[Write a drum-kit groove with actuator coordinates]::
Build an original two-bar groove from simultaneous kick, snare, hi-hat, and
crash interactions.

link:/en/tutorials/oud-bayati/[Compose an original Bayati oud study]::
Keep the oud model, standard tuning, maqam scale table, and contextual pitch
names distinct in a short original exercise.

link:/en/tutorials/bowed-strings/[Combine bowing, slurs, vibrato, and pizzicato]::
Preserve bowed-string phrasing and attack technique on an original violin study.

link:/en/tutorials/nyckelharpa-key-bow/[Coordinate nyckelharpa keys and bow]::
Address keys and bowed strings concurrently, then retain double-stop and drone
intent.

link:/en/tutorials/guitar-techniques/[Author guitar performance techniques]::
Use transition and scoped technique blocks for hammer-ons, pull-offs, bends,
vibrato, tapping, harmonics, palm muting, and slides.

The examples target the `0.1-draft` implementation described in the
link:/en/reference/composer/[Composer Language reference].

Each tutorial includes an inline MIDI player and links to its Composer source,
compiled Universal Tablature JSON, and Standard MIDI File. Maintainers can
regenerate these committed artifacts with `scripts/generate-tutorial-media.sh`.
