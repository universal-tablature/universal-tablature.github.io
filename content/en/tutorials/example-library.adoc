recording, engraving, or MIDI file.

== Reuse policy

For future examples, prefer an original study or a composer whose works are
unambiguously out of copyright in the target jurisdiction. Verify the
composition separately from the source edition: a centuries-old melody can
still appear in a copyrighted arrangement, engraving, recording, or dataset.
Record the composer, work, excerpt, transcription source, and editorial changes
beside every example.
